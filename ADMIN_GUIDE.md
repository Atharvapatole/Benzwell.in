## 2. Managing the Platform

### A. Publishing Digital Products
1. Go to **Products** -> **+ New Product**.
2. Enter the title, price, sale price, category, short summary, what's included list, and target audience.
3. Configure digital file rules: download limits (e.g. 10 downloads) and access duration (e.g. 365 days).
4. Set status to **Published**. The product will immediately appear in the public store catalog.

### B. Visual Block Page Builder
1. Go to **Visual Page Builder**.
2. Select any page (e.g. Homepage, About Us).
3. Use the left element drawer to drag/add sections: Hero headers, Product grids, Category grids, Testimonials, FAQs, or Blog feeds.
4. Customize typography, spacing, and properties in the right inspector panel.
5. Click **Save & Publish** to instantly update the public live page.

### C. Monitoring Orders & Customers
* **Orders:** View real-time Razorpay payment IDs, customer contact details, and item snapshots. Resend fulfillment confirmation emails with one click.
* **Customers:** View customer purchase histories and toggle account access if necessary.
* **Coupons:** Generate percentage or fixed-amount discount codes with expiration dates and usage limits.
* **Security Audit Logs:** Inspect all administrative logins and publishing events.
